Analytics

Advertising

May measure Meta and Google ad performance and build limited audiences. Never includes card, UPI PIN or payment credentials.

Your choice

Use our consent banner/settings when implemented, browser controls, or email [privacy-email] to withdraw optional consent.

Advertising partners

Before launch, configure Meta Pixel/Conversions API and Google Ads/Analytics with consent mode, data minimisation, retention controls and accurate event values. Keep campaign landing pages consistent with the advertised price and availability.

Do not upload customer lists casually

Only upload hashed customer data to advertising platforms when you have a lawful basis, the correct platform terms and the required notices/consent. Never upload payment data or sensitive personal information.

This page describes a responsible setup; it does not itself install Meta or Google tags. Add a consent management mechanism before enabling non-essential tracking.
